What it measures

Numbers you can actually compare.

The same tools and parameters people already post on forums, so a result from your box lines up with everybody else's.

{{template "i-cpu"}}

CPU

openssl sha256 and aes-256-gcm on one thread and on all of them, plus the steal time a busy host takes from your cores while they work.

{{template "i-disk"}}

Disk

fio random read/write at 4k, 64k, 512k and 1m with the same settings as yabs, plus what the disk is: NVMe, SSD, HDD or a virtual disk.

{{template "i-net"}}

Network

iperf3 upload and download, ping and packet loss against {{.Data.Sites}} public servers on five continents, over IPv4 and IPv6.

How it works

Runs anywhere, leaves nothing.

  1. 1{{template "i-term"}}

    Paste the line

    Plain bash and curl, as a normal user. No package manager, no root, no agent.

  2. 2{{template "i-clock"}}

    Wait a few minutes

    Missing tools are fetched as static builds into a temp directory, checked against their sha256 sums and deleted at the end.

  3. 3{{template "i-link"}}

    Read it or share it

    Plain tables in your terminal, plus a link with charts and a preview image. Or keep it local with --no-share.
